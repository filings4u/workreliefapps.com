HOW TO EVALUATE

Start with your business requirements.

Map the workflows

Identify how leads, projects, employees, documents and operational requests move through your team.

Check the actual tools

Review the apps and capabilities you need rather than selecting a platform by its headline app count.

Review total fit

Factor in licensing, onboarding, integrations, security and the work required to maintain the system.
