QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Simple enough to get started. Flexible enough to grow.

Can I pick different apps instead of a fixed bundle?

Yes. You can select any combination of the 19 workrelief applications. The subscription builder calculates the total based on the number of apps selected.

What happens when I add another app?

The pricing builder recalculates your subscription using progressive volume discounts. Review the new total before making changes to your selection.

How much do I save by paying annually?

Annual billing is approximately 7.41% less than twelve monthly payments. The full 19-app suite is $2,500 annually instead of $2,700 across twelve monthly payments.

Can I pay and activate a subscription today?

The current checkout is a subscription preview. Payment processing and production subscription terms are not yet enabled. Contact our team for launch information.
